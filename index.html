Instructions

For Best Results, Please Follow These Instructions:

  • Image Capture: Capture both eyes clearly after 5s, with minimal background.
  • Positioning: Look straight at the camera with both eyes fully open.
  • Eye State: Blink naturally, then keep both eyes fully open for 5 seconds before capture. Avoid shooting immediately after a blink.
  • Remove Obstructions: Remove glasses and contact lenses before capturing the image.
  • Avoid Eye Drops & Rubbing: Do not use eye drops or rub your eyes for at least 30 minutes before capture.
  • Location: All imaging must be performed indoors in a temperature-controlled environment.
  • Clear Image: Ensure the thermal image is sharp, well-framed, and free from motion blur or obstruction.
